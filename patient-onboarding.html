Patient Onboarding Services

Streamline Intake. Improve Patient Experience. Start Care Faster.

Velentra Health provides comprehensive Patient Onboarding Services designed to help healthcare organizations efficiently move patients from referral to active care. Our team manages the administrative and verification steps required to ensure every patient is properly registered, eligible, and ready for service.

What We Handle

Patient Registration & Demographics

  • Accurate collection and entry of patient information
  • Insurance and contact information
  • Responsible party and emergency contact details

Insurance Eligibility & Benefits Verification

  • Verify active insurance coverage
  • Confirm benefits and service coverage
  • Identify deductibles, copays, coinsurance, and limitations
  • Verify Medicaid and Medicare eligibility when applicable

Referral & Documentation Review

  • Review incoming referrals
  • Validate required patient information
  • Identify missing or incomplete documentation
  • Coordinate outstanding records with referral sources

Authorization & Coverage Coordination

  • Identify services requiring prior authorization
  • Track authorization requirements and status
  • Coordinate with payers and referral sources
  • Help prevent delays caused by authorization issues

Patient Intake Coordination

  • Coordinate appointments and initial services
  • Communicate onboarding requirements to patients
  • Ensure required forms and documentation are completed
  • Maintain organized intake records

Compliance & Documentation Readiness

  • Ensure required onboarding documentation is collected
  • Follow payer and organizational requirements
  • Maintain accurate and complete patient records
  • Support HIPAA-conscious handling of patient information

Our Patient Onboarding Workflow

1 Referral Received
2 Patient Registration

4 Benefits Review
5 Authorization Check
6 Documentation Completion
7 Scheduling
8 Ready for Care
